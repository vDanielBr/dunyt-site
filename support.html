Support

Need help with DUNYT? We're here.

Contact us

Email support@dunyt.app. We usually reply within [2 business days].

In the app you can also go to Settings → Support to report a problem or send feedback.

Common questions

I didn't get my confirmation code. Check your spam folder, then tap "Resend code" in the app. Codes expire after one hour.

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen tap "Forgot password?" and enter the code we email you to choose a new password.

Someone is bothering me. Open their profile and block them; they can no longer message you or find you in search. Then tell us at support@dunyt.app.

How do payments work? DUNYT does not process payments. Employers pay Helpers directly, and either side can mark a payment as paid or received to keep records in sync.

How do I delete my account? See Delete your account.
